HCG Foundation — GST status

HCG Foundation Bangalore declares it is not registered under GST and does not have a GSTIN.
Donors / vendors may record the Foundation as an unregistered dealer/customer.
Because it is unregistered, it cannot claim GST input tax credit.

A separate declaration on “Non Applicability of GST Provisions” states the Foundation is not required to take GST registration under Section 22(1) of the CGST Act when aggregate turnover stays below the Act’s threshold, and related Section 24 conditions for compulsory registration are not attracted as described in that letter.

If GST registration becomes required later, the Foundation will register and share GST details with donor companies.
