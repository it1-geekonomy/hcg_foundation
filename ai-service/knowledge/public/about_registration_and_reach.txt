About HCG Foundation — registration, relationship with HCG hospitals, reach and impact

What kind of organisation is HCG Foundation? Under which entity is it registered?
HCG Foundation was constituted and registered as a Public Charitable Trust on 4 December 2006. Its registered office is at Ground Floor, Tower Block, Unity Building Complex, Mission Road, Bangalore 560027, Karnataka, India.
Its registrations: 12A charitable trust registration (effective from 04.12.2006), 80G approval for donor tax deduction, CSR-1 registration (CSR00030677) with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, FCRA registration for foreign contributions, NITI Aayog Darpan ID KA/2017/0162762, and PAN AAATH6254R. It is not registered under GST.

Is HCG Foundation the CSR arm of HCG hospitals? How is it related to HCG?
HCG Foundation is a separate, registered Public Charitable Trust with its own registrations, founded by Dr. B.S. Ajaikumar, its Founder and Managing Trustee, and it does not own HCG hospitals. The website does not say whether it is or is not the CSR arm of HCG hospitals, so this should not be answered with a plain yes or no; for that question, contact hcgfoundation@gmail.com or +91-80-4660-7760.
It works closely with HCG's pan-India hospital network: patients approved for Patient Aid are treated at HCG hospitals, which give them a discount, and the Foundation pays the discounted bill. For CSR programs, HCG hospital partners provide clinical direction and medical expertise, while the Foundation monitors the program and sends quarterly impact reports.

Where does HCG Foundation work? In how many locations or cities is it present?
HCG Foundation is based in Bengaluru. It runs cancer patient aid, early-detection screening camps, equipment and mobile clinic support, and HPV vaccination drives through HCG's pan-India hospital network, so patients are helped at HCG hospitals across India. Mobile screening takes camps to underserved and remote areas, and fundraising events have been held in Bengaluru (Sur Sandhya) and Ahmedabad (Arambh Garba).
An exact number of locations or cities is not published on the website.

How many patients has HCG Foundation helped? What is its impact so far?
According to HCG Foundation's 2026 overview, its journey so far includes:
- 6,500+ patients supported for cancer treatment
- 1,578 cancer awareness and early detection camps
- 85,000+ people engaged in awareness programs
- 4,620 students benefited by school programs
