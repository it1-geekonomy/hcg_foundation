About HCG Foundation — where it works, government institutions, and impact so far

Where does HCG Foundation work? In how many locations or cities is it present?
HCG Foundation is based in Bengaluru. It runs cancer patient aid, early-detection screening camps, equipment and mobile clinic support, and HPV vaccination drives through HCG's pan-India hospital network, so patients are helped at HCG hospitals across India. Mobile screening takes camps to underserved and remote areas, and fundraising events have been held in Bengaluru (Sur Sandhya) and Ahmedabad (Arambh Garba).
An exact number of locations or cities is not published on the website.

Does HCG Foundation work with government institutions, government schools or government hospitals? Does it partner with government organisations or government bodies?
Formal partnerships with government organisations are not published on the website. Its programs do reach government institutions: Student Outreach healthy-habits campaigns run for 4th–6th grade students in government schools since 2018, and HPV vaccination drives have vaccinated girls from government schools in rural Karnataka. For partnership enquiries, contact hcgfoundation@gmail.com or +91-80-4660-7760.

How many patients has HCG Foundation helped? What is its impact so far?
According to HCG Foundation's 2026 overview, its journey so far includes:
- 6,500+ patients supported for cancer treatment
- 1,578 cancer awareness and early detection camps
- 85,000+ people engaged in awareness programs
- 4,620 students benefited by school programs
