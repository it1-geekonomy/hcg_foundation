HCG Foundation mission and programs overview

HCG Foundation is a charitable trust focused on cancer care support in India. Core work includes Patient Aid, awareness and prevention, diagnostics support, community programs, and related projects published on the website.

Psychological / counseling support may be part of holistic patient care where published materials say so.

The Foundation is a trust. It works with HCG hospital facilities for Patient Aid; it does not claim ownership of HCG hospitals.

Non-cancer conditions (for example heart attack or diabetes) are outside the focus of most published materials unless a page explicitly covers them.
