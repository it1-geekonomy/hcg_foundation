HCG Foundation — financial transparency note

HCG Foundation maintains audited financial statements for recent financial years, including materials labeled Financials FY 2021-22, FY 2022-23, and FY 2023-24 in foundation records.

Detailed line-item figures are published through annual report / audited financial channels rather than informal chat summaries. For the latest audited numbers or a copy of financials, use the Annual Reports section on the website or contact hcgfoundation@gmail.com / +91-80-4660-7760.

Do not invent donation totals, expense figures, or surplus amounts unless they appear in indexed published materials.
