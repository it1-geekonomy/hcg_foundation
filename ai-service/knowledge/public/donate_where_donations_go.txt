Donate — where your donation goes, and in-kind support

Where will my donation be used? How are donations spent?
Donations fund HCG Foundation's cancer-care programs:
- Patient Aid (Aasha Daan — The Gift of Hope): financial support for the treatment of economically disadvantaged cancer patients, with a focus on children and young adults. The fund is used case by case, only for patients who show critical financial need.
- Awareness & Screening Camps: early detection of breast, oral and cervical cancers, including mobile screening in remote areas and student outreach.
- Equipment and mobile clinic support, diagnostic support and HPV vaccination projects.
- Swasti Gallery art therapy for patients.
Companies donating through CSR can direct their donation to the program that matches their chosen cause. Money raised at fundraising events goes towards free or heavily reduced-cost cancer treatment for patients who are struggling financially.
Annual reports and audited financials are published on the Annual Reports & Newsletters page (/transparency-and-knowledge-hub).

Does HCG Foundation accept in-kind donations (medicines, equipment, supplies)?
The website does not publish an in-kind donation programme, neither accepting nor refusing it. To offer in-kind support such as medical equipment or supplies, contact HCG Foundation at hcgfoundation@gmail.com or +91-80-4660-7760 to check what is needed. Money donations can be made online through the Donate Now form.
