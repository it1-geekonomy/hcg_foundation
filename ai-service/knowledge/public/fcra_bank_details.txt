HCG Foundation — Foreign / FCRA donations

HCG Foundation is registered under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) and can receive foreign / overseas / NRI contributions.

How foreign donors should proceed:
- Planning to contribute from outside India? Please contact the HCG Foundation Finance Team by email at accounts.hcgf@hcgel.com.
- Our team will guide you through the donation process and share the necessary bank details and instructions to complete your contribution.
- Online international card checkout is not available on the website.
- Do not publish FCRA bank account numbers, IFSC, or SWIFT in chatbot answers for self-serve remittance.

For regular Indian donations, use the website Donate Now flow (Razorpay, INR, with 80G where eligible).
Recurring AutoPay is India / INR only.

Remitter details are required for foreign contributions — anonymous foreign donations cannot be accepted under FCRA.
Do not invent UPI IDs, other account numbers, Branch Code, Sort Code, or cancelled-cheque images as payment instructions.
