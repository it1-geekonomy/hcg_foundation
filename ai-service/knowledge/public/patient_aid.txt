Patient Aid — HCG Foundation

Patient Aid helps eligible cancer patients access treatment support.

How is a patient referred? Referrals come through the treating hospital: doctors and Medical Social Workers identify financially challenged patients and start the assessment. Families with questions can contact the Foundation.

Can I donate for a specific patient, or sponsor / adopt a patient? Sponsor / adopt-a-patient enquiries go through Patient Aid and the official contact channels below; a way to choose a specific patient through the Donate Now form is not published.
